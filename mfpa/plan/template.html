Family Money Plan

A monthly money ledger for the whole family, from take-home pay to goals, home loan and pensions, and the age at which investments could replace salary. Every number comes from the plan file; change it and rebuild.

Planning maths with assumed returns, not a forecast and not financial, tax or insurance advice. The base case uses a steady return; the stress case replays a bad first decade. Neither is a probability.

Investments against what's needed

"Needed" is the smallest amount of investments (in today's rupees) that would last to the end of the plan if the main earner stopped that year, after counting a partner who keeps working, pensions as they start, the loan still to repay and goals still to fund. Goal funds and the emergency fund are not counted as investments.

Yearly milestones

Where the plan expects you each year. Compare it with your real net worth once a year; if you're ahead, the date moves closer.

Monthly budget by phase

Money left after spending goes to the emergency fund first, then the goal SIPs you set, then investments. SIPs are a share of what's left, not extra money.

Goals

Goal funds move to safer debt returns in their last three years. A shortfall is paid from investments on the day and is already counted in the plan above.
